CAMPUS HELPDESK INFORMATION

LEAVE APPLICATION

Students who want to apply for leave should submit a leave request through the student portal.

The student must provide:
Student name
Register number
Department
Leave start date
Leave end date
Reason for leave

For medical leave, the student should upload the required medical certificate.

Students should submit the leave request before taking leave whenever possible.


ATTENDANCE

Students are expected to maintain the required attendance percentage according to college regulations.

Students with attendance shortage should contact their department office for the applicable procedure.


LIBRARY

Students can borrow books using their library account.

Books can be renewed through the library system if the book has not been reserved by another student.


HOSTEL

Hostel students must follow the hostel entry and exit timings specified by the hostel administration.


EXAMINATION

Students should check official college announcements and the student portal for examination notifications and hall-ticket information.
